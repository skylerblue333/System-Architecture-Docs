# ADR-NNN: Decision title

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Superseded | Rejected

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, current evidence, and why a decision is needed. Separate verified facts from assumptions.

## Decision

State the chosen option and the exact scope of the decision.

## Alternatives considered

Document realistic alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Consequences

### Positive

Describe expected benefits.

### Negative / tradeoffs

Describe costs, limitations, operational burden, security implications, and irreversible effects.

## Verification

List the tests, CI evidence, deployment evidence, metrics, or review needed to prove the decision works as intended.

## Rollback / supersession

Explain how the decision can be reversed or what conditions should trigger a new ADR.
